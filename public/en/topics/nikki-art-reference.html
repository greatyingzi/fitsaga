Game Costume as AI Reference: Borrowing Design Language from Nikki-Series Art

FitSaga AI Reference · 2026-11-17
The real workflow of concept artists and fan artists: find reference, break down palette and cut, then create. The Nikki series offers 2,000+ structured 'Chinese fantasy fashion' references — this guide turns them into AI material.

One-line takeaway

The correct use of game costume references is borrowing design language, not copying frames: extract palette, silhouette features, and material combinations — then generate a NEW character in the same design bloodline. That is both creative and copyright-safe.

From suit image to prompt

1. Palette extraction: suit main colors (e.g. Midnight Dance = deep blue + silver + hint of gold) → 'deep blue and silver palette with subtle gold accents'

2. Silhouette features: asymmetric hem / high slit / layered sleeves → 'asymmetric hemline, layered sleeves, thigh slit'

3. Material reading: fabric contrasts in the art (gauze+metal / velvet+embroidery) → apply the fabric vocabulary

4. Atmosphere completion: suit art is usually studio-lit → add 'soft studio lighting, fantasy backdrop'

Three reference intensities (copyright ladder)

· Level 1 · Palette only (safest): entirely new character sharing the color story — commercial-safe

· Level 2 · Silhouette borrowing: similar cut, all-new details — fan-art convention zone

· Level 3 · Frame recreation: character+outfit+pose aligned — personal appreciation / explicit fan-art label only

(Game assets © Papergames — do not commercialize Level 3)

Keywords for the 'concept-art look'

concept art, official art, painterly rendering, clean lineart influence, fantasy character design

Plus 'matte painting background' and the image instantly reads as game key art.

Good vs bad notes

· 'concept art, painterly' gives artbook texture; pure photoreal words turn references into cosplay photos

· One character with a complete design per image; multi-character averages out details

· Props are a concept-art strength: 'holding ornate staff, glowing crystal' worth keeping handy
