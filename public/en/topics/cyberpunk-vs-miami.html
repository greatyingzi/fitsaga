Cyberpunk vs Miami Aesthetic: One Table to Tell Two Neons Apart

FitSaga AI Reference · 2026-11-17
The two most-confused neon styles — one from Blade Runner's dense future city, one from GTA6's Miami dusk. One wrong word and the mood completely drifts.

One-line takeaway

In cyberpunk, neon is the world rule (the city itself is a light source); in Miami aesthetics, neon is decoration (accents at sunset). The 'status' of neon in your prompt decides the style.

The core comparison

| Dimension | Cyberpunk | Miami (GTA6) |

| Sky | permanent haze / purple-green pollution | natural pink-orange-purple sunset |

| Light source |全域 neon + holographic ads | neon signs + sunset glow |

| Streets | wet, high-reflective, steam | damp but natural reflections |

| People | cyberware / techwear | fashionable realism / resort |

| Mood | dystopian pressure | crime-and-freedom romance |

Keyword differences

· Cyberpunk only: cyberpunk city, holographic ads, neon everywhere, dystopian, blade runner atmosphere, techwear

· Miami only: Miami aesthetic, art deco, golden hour, tropical, pastel neon signs, resort vibe

· Shared: wet street reflections, night city, cinematic

Mixing warnings

· cyberpunk + sunset gradient = 'sunset cyberpunk' (actually a great style — but not GTA6)

· Miami + dystopian = mood tearing, avoid

· Want 'cyberpunk at dusk' with visible sky: write 'cyberpunk city at dusk, natural sky visible'

Decision flow

1. Main light from the sky? → Miami direction

2. Main light from man-made sources (billboards/holo)? → Cyberpunk

3. Both? → name the dominant source explicitly

FAQ

Is GTA6 cyberpunk?

No. It's modern-crime-romance aesthetics; neon is decoration. Blade Runner / Ghost in the Shell are the canon.

Can the two styles merge?

Yes with explicit dominance — 'dominant light source'. Fusion often yields unique results.
