Chinese New Year AI Images: From New-Year Outfits to Neo-Chinese Editorial

FitSaga AI Reference · 2026-11-16
CNY is the annual traffic peak for Chinese-style visuals (family looks, ancient-town shoots, neo-Chinese fashion). Three scene recipes with prompt formulas, pitfalls and model params — bookmark before the holidays.

One-line takeaway

The viral CNY formula = red-dominant palette × scene symbols (lanterns/couplets/snow) × human narrative (looking back / holding lantern / holding fan). All three make a festival editorial; without scene symbols it's just 'a woman in red'.

Three scenes

1 · New-year family look (indoor warmth)

Chinese New Year outfit, red traditional dress, lanterns, spring couplets, warm indoor lighting

· Warm indoor light is the soul — red outdoors reads costume, red indoors reads reunion

2 · Ancient-town travel shoot (grand outdoor)

ancient Chinese town, snow, red lanterns hanging, cobblestone street, hanfu flowing

· Add snow: red-on-snow contrast explodes; without snow you get the grey-blue Jiangnan school

3 · Neo-Chinese daily (modern fusion)

modern chinese fashion, mandarin collar, red accent outfit, minimalist, editorial

· Red only as accent (one piece or accessory) — the prompt-ified version of viral RED posts

Pose vocabulary (the hardest part)

· Looking back: looking back over shoulder, graceful turn

· Lantern: holding a red lantern, soft glow on face

· Fan: holding a round silk fan, half-covering face

· Sparkler: holding sparkler, sparkler light on face (night-scene finishing touch)

· Greeting: cupped hands greeting gesture, slight bow (don't write 'praying')

Good vs bad notes

· 'chinese new year' alone generates cartoon red-envelope piles — pair with 'editorial photography' or 'cinematic still'

· Red overload: write 'deep red dominant, other colors muted'

· Hair is the hidden failure: traditional chinese hairstyle, hairpins mandatory, or you get modern hair mixing in

Model params

· Midjourney v7: --ar 3:4 (portrait) or 16:9 (scene), --style raw

· FLUX: natural sentences, guidance 3.2 — best hand details

· Jimeng: Chinese keywords '春节 国风 古镇 雪 红灯笼 回眸' hit directly
