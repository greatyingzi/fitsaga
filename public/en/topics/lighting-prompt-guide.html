AI Lighting Prompt Guide: Four Types of Light and How They Differ

FitSaga AI Reference · 2026-11-16
Lighting is the watershed between 'photographic' and 'plastic' AI images — and the most transferable knowledge across styles. Four light recipes, any subject, instant quality lift.

One-line takeaway

The 'expensive look' is 80% lighting precision: direction (backlit/side) + time (golden/blue hour) + quality (diffused/hard) — a 3-axis coordinate beats vague words like 'beautiful lighting' by an order of magnitude.

The four workhorse lights

1 · Golden Hour (universal for portraits)

golden hour, warm rim light, sun flare, long soft shadows, glowing backlight on hair

· Rim light is what makes hair glow — without it subjects look pasted onto backgrounds

2 · Blue Hour (pre-night city)

blue hour, city lights just turned on, deep blue sky, cool ambient light

· The 20 minutes after sunset: deep blue sky + fresh lights = peak urban mood

3 · Window Light (indoor portraits)

soft window light, diffused daylight, light falloff, gentle shadows, Rembrandt lighting

· Rembrandt lighting (triangle on the cheek) is a one-word quality upgrade

4 · Studio (commercial look)

studio lighting, softbox, seamless backdrop, high key / low key

· High key suits sweet styles; low key suits goth/power looks

Light × style quick table

| Style | Best light | Bonus words |

| Hanfu | window / golden side-back | dust motes |

| Baddie | hard low-angle | harsh shadows |

| Sweet | high-key soft | pastel tones, airy |

| Goth | low-key single lamp | chiaroscuro |

| Street | overcast diffused | even light |

Good vs bad notes

· 'dramatic lighting' alone is empty — 'single window light from left, dramatic falloff' is controllable

· Don't mix 'natural light' with studio words

· Backlit portraits need 'backlight on hair' or faces go black

Pitfalls

· 'cinematic lighting' does nothing alone; pair with direction words

· Night portrait two-light trick: neon sign as key light, streetlamp as rim — naming concrete sources is the secret

FAQ

Why do my images look plastic?

Missing or vague light words 90% of the time. Add diffusion/direction/falloff words immediately.

Do light words behave the same across models?

Midjourney is direction-sensitive; FLUX obeys natural sentences; SDXL often needs LoRA help.
